About Green Valley

A school community built on care, curiosity, and high expectations.

Green Valley Public School helps students grow into thoughtful, capable, and confident young people through meaningful relationships, strong teaching, and a campus culture that celebrates effort as much as achievement.

Mission and Values

We aim to develop students who are academically strong, ethically grounded, and ready to contribute positively to the world around them.

  • Integrity, empathy, and respect in every interaction
  • Curiosity-led learning supported by clear expectations
  • Leadership opportunities that encourage initiative

Student Experience

Daily school life blends classroom rigor with celebration, collaboration, house activities, and opportunities for performance and participation.

  • Assemblies, competitions, exhibitions, and annual events
  • Age-appropriate pastoral care and student wellbeing support
  • Mentoring that helps learners discover strengths early
