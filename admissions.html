Helpful Checklist

Keeping a few details ready will make the initial inquiry faster and smoother for your family.

  • Student name and current grade information
  • Preferred entry class and contact number
  • Questions about transport, academics, or campus life

What Happens Next

Once your inquiry is received, our team will review the request and connect with you regarding the next available admissions interaction.

  • Confirmation from the admissions office
  • Guidance on visits, interaction rounds, and documentation
  • Support throughout enrollment and onboarding

Campus interactions are open by appointment.

Families are encouraged to visit the school, meet our team, and ask practical questions about programs, facilities, and student life before completing enrollment.
