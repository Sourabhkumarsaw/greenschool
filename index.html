About Us

A school community designed for curious learners.

At Green Valley Public School, we create a safe and inspiring environment where students can think deeply, communicate clearly, and discover their strengths.

  • Project-based learning across core subjects
  • Mentorship culture that supports personal growth
  • Balanced development through arts, sports, and leadership

Academics

Learning pathways built for every stage of school life.

Our academic model is structured, engaging, and future-focused, helping students build mastery while staying curious and creative.

Strong foundations with joyful discovery

Early learners build confidence in language, numeracy, collaboration, and expression through activity-based teaching.

  • Reading readiness and communication skills
  • Creative exploration through music and art
  • Confidence-building classroom routines

Concept mastery with guided independence

Middle school students strengthen analytical thinking, teamwork, and digital literacy through deeper subject engagement.

  • STEM labs and interdisciplinary projects
  • Club participation and communication practice
  • Regular feedback that supports growth

Focused preparation for university and beyond

Senior learners receive academic rigor, career guidance, and leadership opportunities that prepare them for competitive futures.

  • Advanced subject mentoring and exam support
  • Career counseling and portfolio building
  • Leadership programs and public speaking exposure
